Privacy notice

Last updated 8 November 2026.

Who are we?

Wájẹun is a Nigerian food and culture brand run by Damilola Orija in the United Kingdom. You can contact us at hellowajeun@gmail.com.

What information do we collect?

Only what you type into our sign-up boxes: your email address. We also record the date, which page you signed up on, and that you agreed to hear from us.

Why do we collect it?

To email you about the thing you signed up for. That means news of our food events, the opening of private chef bookings, or the launch of our Nigerian food calorie tool. We only email you about the topic you chose, and we ask for your consent before we do.

Where is it stored and who can see it?

Your email is stored in a private Google Sheet in our Google account. Only we can open it. Google processes the data for us and may do so outside the UK under its standard safeguards. We never sell or share your email with anyone else.

How long do we keep it?

Until you ask us to remove it or you unsubscribe. We review the list every 12 months and delete addresses that are no longer needed.

What are your rights?

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold, correct it, delete it, or stop emailing you. Every email we send will include a way to unsubscribe. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Does this website use cookies?

No. We do not use cookies or tracking tools. The site loads its font from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google when a page loads.
